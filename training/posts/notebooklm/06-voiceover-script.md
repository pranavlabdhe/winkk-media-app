# Social Posts module — voiceover script

Companion narration for `video/posts-walkthrough.mp4` (about 1 min 42 s).

- **Timing:** the times below match `storyboard/timings.json`. Each step appears roughly when
  its segment starts. Record one segment per step, then align them to the screenshot cut.
- **Pace:** about 150 words per minute. If a line runs long, cut words, not screens.
- **Tone:** calm, direct, one colleague showing another. No hype.
- **Never say:** passwords, email addresses, or counts that could change.

---

### Step 1 — The post board (0:07)

> This is Social Posts: the publishing calendar for every client. One row per post, across the
> whole agency.

### Step 2 — Status chips (0:14)

> The chips count the week: scheduled, published, pending, and missed. One glance tells you what
> still has to go out.

### Step 3 — Filter by status (0:21)

> Click a status to narrow the list. The filter stays in the URL, so you can send the same view
> to a teammate.

### Step 4 — Filter by client (0:28)

> From a client page, choose Manage under Social posts. Now you see one client's calendar, and
> the counts narrow with it.

### Step 5 — One post row (0:34)

> Each row answers three questions: what is going out, when, and which task owns the work.

### Step 6 — The linked task (0:41)

> The linked task carries the owner, the task status, and the due date. This post is scheduled,
> and the task behind it is in progress with the Social Media Executive.

### Step 7 — Add a post (0:48)

> To add one, use the form at the top: client, title, status, platform, schedule, and the
> caption the team needs.

### Step 8 — Choose the task (0:54)

> For the task, link an existing one, or leave the default. Leaving it blank lets the app create
> the task for you.

### Step 9 — The example (1:01)

> Here I add the Solhaus Diwali Offer Story, scheduled for 17 November, with a short caption.
> The client is already selected because I am on the Solhaus view.

### Step 10 — Created together (1:09)

> One click saves the post and its task. The new row already shows the linked task, due the same
> day, in progress.

### Step 11 — Publish (1:16)

> When the post goes live, set it to published, add the publish date, and click Save. That is the
> only update I make.

### Step 12 — The task follows (1:24)

> The linked task is now done. Published maps to done, automatically. Nobody opened Tasks to
> change it.

### Step 13 — Confirm on the task (1:33)

> Open the task to confirm: created from the social post, the post listed as published, and the
> sync written into the activity history.

### Step 14 — Recap (1:40)

> The post is the publishing record. The task is the work record. Publish once, and both update.
> That is Social Posts.

## Optional 30-second cut

> Social Posts is the publishing calendar. Filter by status or client, read one row, and you
> know what is going out and who owns the work. Add a post, let the app create the linked task,
> then publish it. The task becomes done by itself. The post is the publishing record; the task
> is the work record. Publish once, and both update.

## Recording notes

- Record one step per take, then trim. Leave half a second of silence at each end.
- If you re-run `npm run training:posts`, re-check `timings.json`; the spacing may shift by a
  second or two.
- Keep the browser at 100% zoom. The MP4 is scaled to 1280 px wide.
- The example is recorded, then removed from the database. Do not read the post id or task id
  aloud; they change on every run.
