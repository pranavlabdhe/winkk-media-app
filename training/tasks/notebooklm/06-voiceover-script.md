# Tasks module — voiceover script

Companion narration for `video/tasks-walkthrough.mp4` (about 1 min 40 s).

- **Timing:** the times below match `storyboard/timings.json`. Each step appears roughly when
  its segment starts. Record one segment per step, then align them to the screenshot cut.
- **Pace:** about 150 words per minute. If a line runs long, cut words, not screens.
- **Tone:** calm, direct, one colleague showing another. No hype.
- **Never say:** passwords, email addresses, or counts that could change.

---

### Step 1 — Board overview (0:05)

> This is the Tasks board. Every piece of client work starts here: a reel, a design, a
> meeting follow-up, a client request.

### Step 2 — Filters (0:12)

> Use the filters to find work. Client, teammate, category, status.

### Step 3 — Filters in use (0:19)

> Here I ask one question: what social work is still in progress for Solhaus?

### Step 4 — Filtered result (0:27)

> Only matching tasks remain. Filters combine, so you can narrow to one client, one person,
> one stage.

### Step 5 — Create a task (0:34)

> To add work, use the create form. Name the work: “Solhaus Diwali Campaign Reel”. Then choose
> the category.

### Step 6 — Link an existing record (0:41)

> The category decides which module connects. If that post or meeting already exists, link it
> here. Do not create a second copy.

### Step 7 — One task in focus (0:49)

> Open one task. One owner, one client, one category, one due date, one status. This is the
> record everyone works from.

### Step 8 — Linked records (0:56)

> These are the linked records. The Social Post and the client message are the same work,
> shown in their own modules.

### Step 9 — Activity history (1:03)

> This is the activity history. Every status change and note is recorded, with who did it and
> when.

### Step 10 — Move to Done (1:10)

> When the work is finished, set the status to Done. Then click Update. That is the only
> update you make.

### Step 11 — The automatic sync (1:19)

> Watch the linked record. The Social Post is now Published — automatically. Nobody opened
> Social Posts. The task drives the module.

### Step 12 — History entry (1:29)

> The change is written into the history, and appears on the client timeline with every other
> event for Solhaus.

### Step 13 — Recap (1:36)

> Three habits. Create the work once as a task. Update the task, not five places. Add a note
> when the status changes. That is the Tasks module.

## Optional 30-second cut

> Every client task lives on one board. Filter it, create it, open it. When you mark the task
> Done, the linked social post becomes Published by itself. One update, everywhere. Create it
> once as a task, and update the task — not five places.

## Recording notes

- Record one step per take, then trim. Leave half a second of silence at each end.
- If you re-run `npm run training:tasks`, re-check `timings.json`; the spacing may shift by a
  second or two.
- Keep the browser at 100% zoom. The MP4 is scaled to 1280 px wide.
